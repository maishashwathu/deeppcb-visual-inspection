- Raw data, the upstream repository, and model weights are not redistributed here. The notebook downloads the pinned upstream archive and trains locally.

## Algorithms

This project combines established algorithms; it does not claim to invent HOG, ORB, SIFT, RANSAC or random forests.

- Dalal and Triggs, *Histograms of Oriented Gradients for Human Detection*, CVPR 2005. [Paper](https://lear.inrialpes.fr/people/triggs/pubs/Dalal-cvpr05.pdf).
- Lowe, *Distinctive Image Features from Scale-Invariant Keypoints*, IJCV 2004. [Paper](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~lowe/papers/ijcv04.pdf).
- Rublee et al., *ORB: An efficient alternative to SIFT or SURF*, ICCV 2011. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV.2011.6126544).
- Fischler and Bolles, *Random Sample Consensus*, CACM 1981. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/358669.358692).
- Breiman, *Random Forests*, Machine Learning 2001. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1010933404324).

## Project contribution and AI assistance

Prepared for Shashwat Jain's Computer Vision for AI coursework, with implementation and writing assistance from **OpenAI Codex**. The project-specific contribution is the integrated CPU inspection pipeline, reproducible split and evaluation protocol, controlled alignment comparison, explanatory visuals and educational materials. It is not a reproduction of the original paper's neural network.

The reported metrics are generated by running this implementation. Upstream headline benchmark scores are not presented as this project's results. The student should review, rerun, understand and explain the work, and follow the faculty's disclosure requirements.

The course policy PDF is not redistributed. Course-outcome descriptions in the guide are paraphrased for project planning.
